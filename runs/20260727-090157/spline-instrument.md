# Spline exact instrument

The original `scene.splinecode` is loaded by the official Spline runtime pinned at `1.12.98`.

Preserved by construction:

- geometry and hierarchy
- original materials, lighting, camera, animation, and events
- pointer and touch behavior
- discoverable `morph`, `flow`, and `heat` variables, matched case-insensitively

Runtime API: `window.RelayExactRuntime`.
